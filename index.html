Frequently Asked Questions
How do I play games on Gryo? ▼
Just click on any game card on the homepage. No login or download required — it launches instantly in your browser.
Is Gryo free to use? ▼
Yes! Gryo offers free access to basic games. You can unlock premium features with our Plus or Pro plans.
Can I use Gryo at school? ▼
Absolutely. Gryo is designed to be school-friendly and lightweight for classroom environments.
What is included in the Pro School plan? ▼
The Pro School plan offers unlimited access, custom branding, and a license tailored for school-wide deployment.
